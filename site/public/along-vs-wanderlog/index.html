along vs Wanderlog

Wanderlog and along are both trip planners, but they are built for different jobs. Wanderlog is a detailed itinerary and map planner. along is built for groups who share a plan and shared costs. Here is how they differ.

alongWanderlog
Built mainly forA group sharing one plan, costs and memoriesDetailed itinerary and map planning, including solo trips
ItineraryDay-by-day plan with a map for each place, edit, move and undoItinerary and map in one view, with place suggestions and route tools
Group expense splittingFour split methods (equal, amounts, percent, shares), balances in plain words, fewest payments to settleBudgeting and expense tracking is part of the planner
Friends without the appAdded by name, included in plans and splits, can join laterCollaborators use the app or website
Booking importNot offeredImport bookings by email forwarding
MemoriesPhotos, notes, Google Photos links and travel stampsSee wanderlog.com for its current options
Offline expense entryYes, saved on your phone and synced laterSee wanderlog.com for its offline options

Choose Wanderlog if

  • You want a detailed, map-first itinerary with place suggestions and route tools.
  • You want to pull flight and hotel bookings into the plan from your email.
  • You mostly plan alone, or do not need to split costs.

Choose along if

  • You travel with friends or family and need everyone on one plan.
  • You want clear, exact answers to “who paid what” and “who owes whom”.
  • Some friends will not install an app and should still be in the plan.
  • You want to keep memories and collect travel stamps after the trip.

Can you use both?

Yes. Some groups research and map the route in a detailed planner, then run the trip, the costs and the memories in along.

Frequently asked questions

Is along a good Wanderlog alternative for groups?

If your main need is a shared plan with exact expense splitting and friends who may not have the app, along is built for that. If you want booking import and detailed route tools, Wanderlog is stronger there.

Does along import flight and hotel bookings?

No. along keeps the plan, costs and memories, but does not import bookings.

Is along free?

Yes, it is free to start on iPhone and Android.
