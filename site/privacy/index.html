Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-09-30

What along keeps

Your name and email from your sign-in, the trips you join or create, and what you add to them: plans, expenses, payments and memories.

Who can see it

Only the people on a trip can see that trip. Nobody outside it can, and along does not sell your data or show adverts.

Your choices

You can pick which notifications you get, set your country, and log out at any time. You can delete your account from Profile.

Deleting your account

Your name becomes Deleted user, your sign-in is removed, and your notifications are erased. Expenses and payments you shared stay on the trip so everyone else's balances remain correct.
